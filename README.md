It's just Voice Assistant with AI for basic usings.

Its functionality:
1. Open basic apps (calc, cmd, explorer, vs code, notepad)
2. Search an information in web
3. Change your music
4. Turn up and turn down your volume
5. Show your time now
6. Use AI to answer your question
7. Show your date now
